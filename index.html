Our Story

A Neighbourhood Café Built on Slow Mornings

Brew & Bloom started as a tiny corner counter with one espresso machine and a stubborn belief that good coffee shouldn't be rushed. Nine years later, we still roast in small batches, still know our regulars by name, and still believe the best conversations happen over a second cup.

  • Roasted in-house — every batch dated and cupped before it hits the bar.
  • Baked before sunrise — croissants laminated by hand, never frozen.
  • Direct trade — we buy from twelve farms across four countries.

— Meera & Arjun, founders
